Q1 7 Marks Unit 1: Water Technology
Explain EDTA method for determination of hardness of water with complete chemical reactions and principle.
👨‍🏫 Tailored for: Dr. M. Sharma (Flowchart & Step Marks Active) Pedagogical Rubric
📘 Part 1: Introduction & Theoretical Foundation

Hardness in water is the measure of capacity to precipitate soap, predominantly caused by divalent calcium (Ca²⁺) and magnesium (Mg²⁺) cations. Disodium EDTA behaves as a hexadentate chelating ligand that forms highly stable 1:1 soluble complexes at pH 9-10.

🏷️ Part 2: Concept Classification & Taxonomy
Temporary (Carbonate) Hardness

Caused by Ca(HCO₃)₂ and Mg(HCO₃)₂. Decomposes upon simple boiling into insoluble carbonates.

Permanent (Non-Carbonate) Hardness

Caused by chlorides and sulfates (CaCl₂, MgSO₄). Cannot be removed by boiling; requires EDTA chelation or ion exchange.

🔢 Part 3: Step-by-Step Derivation & Reactions
Indicator reaction: [Ca²⁺ / Mg²⁺] + EBT (Steel Blue) → [M-EBT] (Wine Red Unstable Complex)
Titration reaction: [M-EBT] + EDTA → [M-EDTA] (Stable Colorless) + Free EBT (Steel Blue at Endpoint)
Boxed University Formulation: Total Hardness = [V_EDTA × M × 1000] / V_sample ppm CaCO3
🏭 Part 4: Real-World & Industrial Applications
  • Thermal power boiler feed water softening to prevent hazardous scale deposition and tube blowout accidents.
  • Municipal water treatment facility hardness titration and potable standard certification.
  • Industrial laundry and textile dying water conditioning to reduce detergent wastage.
